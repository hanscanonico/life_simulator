SUMMARY: DOES DEPTH GAINED LATE COME WITH NEW CODE OR WITH CO-OPTION?
Neither answer holds across the board. The answer depends on the arm and on how the step is counted.
- In the rise arm (where depth pays), the one late rise (5137, depth 7 to 10) comes with new code at the
  minimum margin: 13 to 15 bytes. It is not a climb past the arm's plateau. 38 of 54 rise children end
  at depth 10 in their last world, and 36 were already at 10 in their fifth-decile world. 5137 relapsed
  early (settled relapse at 67 470) and re-climbed to the arm's common depth 10. Its last solver
  `.-[{[,~{!~>~>,]` carries the same loop core, `,~{!~>~>`, as the other rise children's depth-10
  solvers (exactly in 5017, 5104 and 5143; with `!{` swapped in 5005 and 5161).
- In the capped arm (the late gains there are steps the cap does not pay for), the split is 6 new code
  and 7 co-option. Two of the six new-code children climb from ECHO in their fifth-decile world: 5114
  goes from 0/2 to 7/12 and 5117 from 0/7 to 7/12. Those are re-climbs after a collapse, not a step
  from one deep rung to the next. Counting only steps from a deep base (fifth-world depth 4 or more)
  leaves 4 new code (each +2 bytes) and 7 co-option (0 to -2 bytes). So the capped arm's late depth
  leans to co-option.
- What the bytes show, read off the printed solvers (descriptive, not part of the lock). In deep worlds
  the load-bearing bytes are a contiguous prefix of the tape that ends at a loop's closing `]`.
  - All nine capped late risers that end at depth 8 carry the same 10-byte loop, `{,~[{~>~!]`:
    5018, 5096, 5099, 5132, 5147, 5162 and 5165 exactly, 5069 with an extra `[` (`{[,~[{~>~!]`), and
    5150 reordered (`{,~{[~>~{!]`).
  - The depth-7 loop is typically `{,[~{!~>]` or `{,[~!{~>]`, 9 bytes. Its symbols are a reordering of the depth-8
    loop with one `~` fewer.
  - Each child's count is the loop plus the bytes in front of it. The +2 / 0 / -2 swing that separates
    new code from co-option in the 7-to-8 steps comes mostly from how many bearing bytes stand before
    the loop. The loop itself grows by about one byte.
  - The parents are correlated (three children each of 18 worlds), so this one loop recurring across
    parents may be shared ancestry, not convergence.
- Noise floor for the +2 threshold: of 91 children whose fifth and last worlds hold the same depth
  (above ECHO), the count changes by 0 in 74, by +1 or -1 in 13, and by 2 or more in either direction
  in 4 (+2 or more in 2). A +2 is beyond typical drift, but only just.
- Across all 486 worlds, the load-bearing count rises with depth: median 10 at depth 7 (n 119), 12 at
  depth 8 (n 50), 14 at depth 10 (n 83). Deeper solvers are longer code. But in the late one-rung steps
  the loop gains about one byte every time, and whether the label reads new code or co-option turns on
  the bytes in front of it.
- In one line: late depth in the paid arm is a single re-climb to the arm's plateau with +2 bytes. Late
  depth in the capped arm is mostly a one-rung step (7 to 8) onto one recurring 10-byte loop: about
  half co-option, about half new code at the minimum margin. That does not support a sustained growth
  of code with depth.

CAVEATS
- The dominant deepest solver is one exact tape held by only 4 to 25 cells (median 9) of 16 384.
  Each world is a cloud of near-variants, and the count reads one member of it.
- The world rule and the decile rule can disagree. For 5137 the fifth-decile median is 0 (the bar of 7
  comes from first-half persistence), but its 110 000 world already holds 7. The label is judged on the
  worlds, as locked.
- Cross-check: at the same epoch, the tool's depth agrees with the engine's own logic_depth_max sample
  in 433 of 486 worlds. 48 of the 53 disagreements are at the ECHO/nothing boundary (0 against -1),
  mostly in the none arm. The other 5 are FIRST worlds during the re-climb (5009 1/0, 5063 2/1,
  5068 2/3, 5074 2/1, 5144 5/7, 5152 6/2), where the engine draws its case sets at random. At every
  FIFTH and LAST world holding depth 1 or more, the two agree.
- One substrate, one seed per parent, correlated parents, and an imported objective, primitive,
  channel, semantics and ladder (the entry's "not claimed"). This reading is descriptive, tested
  nowhere, and moves no outcome.
