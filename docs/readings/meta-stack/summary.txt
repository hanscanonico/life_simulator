Meta-stack sweep: the locked offline readings for the 9 deep children
=====================================================================
These readings were made on 2026-10-02 with research/landscape at main 3cd9abb, built out of tree. The stored
worlds and params came from the lab through SELECTs only. The method follows docs/design_record.md (2026-10-02,
"Reported with each deep child") and research/landscape/README.md.

First epochs follow the entry's persistence rule: the first own sample of 5 consecutive samples at a share of at
least 1/10, over every sample past parent_epoch 20000. They are recomputed from logic_share_* and equal the final
report's first_* columns exactly. E_deep is the earlier of first_xor and first_equ. E_last is the latest first
epoch among the 8 rungs below XOR that reached the line at or before E_deep.

TOTALS
------
Multi-step crossings, by the locked rule d >= 2: 9 of 9 (d = 17 to 31).
Heritability, by child: 0 heritable, 8 unresolved, 1 not heritable (4979).
By planting, that is 15 locked plantings plus 1 extension: 15 Unresolved and 1 NotHeritable. In every Unresolved
planting, every seed's control was back at a tenth by e+500, at 5889 to 10043 cells, level with the planted world.
The one exception is 4890's xor extension, where only seed 2003's control (1758) reached the tenth.
4979: neither world reached a tenth. The planted world held 55 to 589 cells and the control 167 to 759.

Credited order: none in any child. With more than 16 sites the tool reads no lattice. A credited order gains at
least 1 paid unit a step, so it needs a gain of at least d. In every child the deep solver earns fewer than d
units more than the dominant tape (gains 0 to 22 against d 17 to 31), so no credited order is possible.
Neutral-or-better order: yes in 4863, 4979 and 4980; no in the other 6. This is an exhaustive search of the
subset lattice (helper/, orders/).

DESCRIPTIVE CONTEXT (not locked, not the entry's; read before quoting "9 of 9")
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- The locked distance is dominated by drift. Each world holds 9634 to 14375 distinct metabolism tapes out of
  16384, and its dominant tape sits on 9 to 21 cells. That dominant tape is a median 22 to 30 bytes (of 32) from a
  cell of its own world. In the pre world, the tape nearest the first deep solver is 13 to 23 substitutions away,
  and no cell is within 2 of it in any child. At meta_rate 1/256 per byte, a 32-byte tape takes about 0.125 hits an
  epoch, so any two stored worlds 1000 epochs apart differ by many more than 2 substitutions. The ">= 2" cut cannot
  come out "no" except where the dominant tape is the deep solver itself.
- The study's own view: `paths DOMINANT --k 3`, every mutant within 3 substitutions over the 14-symbol alphabet.
  - nearest deep mutant at 2: 4845, 4871
  - at 3: 4863, 4952, 4980
  - none within 3: 4862, 4961
  - already deep: 4890, 4979
  - no credited path within 3 in any child.
- Fewest of the d substitutions toward the first deep solver that make the dominant tape deep:
  - 2: 4871
  - 4: 4980
  - 5: 4863
  - more than 7: 4845, 4862, 4952, 4961
  - 0, already deep: 4890, 4979
- Why the plantings come out unresolved: the filler (the commonest tape credited the rung on no set) is one
  substitution from the rung in 10 of 16 plantings (9 of them by a credited step). Wiping 2976 to 8601 carriers does not
  stop the rung re-arising within 500 epochs. This is the case the entry foresaw: stepping stones sit one
  substitution below, and a given substitution at a given site comes about every 3600 cell-epochs.

INTERPRETATIONS OF THE LOCKED METHOD
------------------------------------
1. The deep rung's first epoch follows the entry's k = 5 rule (the report's first_xor and first_equ). The brief's
   "first settled deep epoch" column is something else: the first single sample with logic_capability_deep > 0,
   with no persistence. The two choices give different post worlds for 4952 (e29000 under the entry, e28000 under
   the brief) and 4979 (e42000 against e40000).
   Sensitivity (sensitivity/):
   - 4952 at e28000: d = 28, still multi-step.
   - 4979 at e40000: the commonest deep solver is the dominant tape itself, so d = 0 and it is not multi-step. The
     tally would be 8 of 9 under the brief's epochs.
2. "The last lower rung to reach the line" counts only rungs that reached it at or before E_deep. 4871's NAND
   first reaches the line at 24430, after its deep rung at 23180. Taken literally, it would put the "pre-deep"
   world at e24000, after the deep rung, where the dominant tape is the deep solver (d = 0). 4961's NOR (21730,
   after XOR at 21630) is excluded the same way, but the world is e21000 either way.
3. The dominant tape is the census's commonest metabolism tape, ties broken by byte order, with no credit
   condition, as written. In 4890 and 4979 it is already credited EQU on all 6 sets at the last lower rung's
   line. Their EQU share in the samples was below a tenth until later. The locked rule still reads d >= 2 as
   multi-step for them, though no rung was crossed between the two tapes.
4. Orders: the tool's lattice stops at 16 sites. I excluded credited orders by the unit bound above. I decided
   neutral-or-better by an exhaustive level search, with the tool's own definition (no tape along the path earns
   fewer paid units than the start) and the landscape crate's own Scorer, in a scratchpad helper (helper/). It
   evaluated up to 40 M subsets for 4952.
5. Planting per rung: the README says "once per deep rung it reached"; the entry speaks of "that deep solver" and
   "the rung". I planted the first deep solver once for each deep rung that the child reached (k = 5) and that the
   solver carries.
   - 4890 reached XOR at 28290, but its first deep solver (e23000) carries EQU only. As a labelled extension, I
     planted XOR's own first solver (the commonest XOR-credited tape at e29000, credited nand+orn+xor+equ). The
     child's verdict is from EQU.
   - Where a child has two rungs, both read the same, so no combining rule was needed.
6. The planted replicating tape follows the README: that of the first cell, in row order, holding the deep solver
   in the post world. The entry says only "its cell's".
7. A filler may carry the other deep rung: 4890's EQU filler is credited XOR, and its XOR filler EQU. The entry
   allows this ("credited it on none" refers to the planted rung).
I found no contradiction between the README and the entry. The README adds the per-rung planting and the
first-cell replicating tape. The entry does not lock the orders; only the brief asks for them.
